Privacy policy.

Effective August 25, 2026

This notice covers the Secret Satoshis websites at secretsatoshis.com, dashboard.secretsatoshis.com, and charts.secretsatoshis.com. They are built to be explored without creating an account or providing personal information.

Information this website collects

These websites do not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, account systems, or their own subscriber database. Their hosting providers may process standard request information—such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, and request time—to deliver and secure the sites.

Newsletter subscriptions

The newsletter form sends the email address you enter directly to Secret Satoshis on Substack. Secret Satoshis does not receive or store that form submission through this website. Substack processes subscriptions under its own terms and privacy policy.

Public data and external services

The site reads public Bitcoin data from the Secret Satoshis public repositories and links to external services including GitHub, ChatGPT, Substack, LinkedIn, and X. Those services may collect information under their own policies when you visit them.

Sale or sharing of personal information

Secret Satoshis does not sell personal information collected through this website. Because the site does not operate its own accounts, analytics, or advertising system, it does not build behavioral profiles of visitors.

Policy updates

This notice may change if the website's features or service providers change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Questions

Questions about this notice can be sent through @SecretSatoshis on X.
