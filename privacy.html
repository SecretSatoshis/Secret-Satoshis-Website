Privacy policy.

Updated October 9, 2026

This notice covers the Secret Satoshis websites at secretsatoshis.com, dashboard.secretsatoshis.com, charts.secretsatoshis.com, and agent21.secretsatoshis.com. Public research and dashboards can be explored without an account. The Agent 21 invited beta requires an account.

Information this website collects

The public pages do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Agent 21 uses account and session cookies to authenticate invited users. Their hosting providers may process standard request information—such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, and request time—to deliver and secure the sites.

Newsletter subscriptions

The newsletter form sends the email address you enter directly to Secret Satoshis on Substack. Secret Satoshis does not receive or store that form submission through this website. Substack processes subscriptions under its own terms and privacy policy.

Public data and external services

The site reads public Bitcoin data from the Secret Satoshis public repositories on GitHub. On the homepage, the Agent 21 example also reads the current block height and the bitcoin mined so far from the Bitcoin Research Kit (BRK, at bitview.space), directly from your browser, so BRK receives standard request information such as your IP address. The site links to external services including GitHub, Substack, LinkedIn, and X. Those services may collect information under their own policies when you visit them.

Sale or sharing of personal information

Secret Satoshis does not sell personal information collected through this website. The site does not use chat content to build advertising profiles.

Agent 21 accounts and conversations

Clerk manages sign-in, email verification, and account security. We associate your Clerk user identifier with beta access, conversations, files, and usage limits. OpenAI processes your messages, relevant history, research, tool results, and uploaded files to generate responses in its managed agent environment.

Neon Postgres stores your chat history, conversation titles, archive status, and feedback, along with ownership, provider identifiers, run status, file records, diagnostics, and deletion jobs. It also stores temporary message-submission payloads and response snapshots used for recovery and duplicate prevention; these operational records can contain user-related content. Message-submission payloads are cleared when a run settles, and response snapshots are removed through conversation or account cleanup. Private Vercel Blob storage retains your uploads, charts, and other generated files. Vercel hosts the application and runs its background jobs. These processors may retain request and security logs under their own policies. Their authorized administrators, and the Secret Satoshis administrator, may access stored content for support, operations, and abuse investigation; chats are not end-to-end encrypted.

Files, tools, and retention

Conversations and retained files remain until you delete them. Temporary OpenAI sandboxes expire; their expiration does not delete retained conversations or files. Agent 21 retrieves public data from the Bitcoin Research Kit (BRK, at bitview.space), Polymarket, GitHub, and other public websites. It reads GitHub through GitHub's read-only data service, and its analysis sandbox downloads public data directly, such as BRK's Bitcoin series and network data, Polymarket markets, and Secret Satoshis datasets. To find current news it uses OpenAI's hosted web search. Agent 21 is instructed to send only what an answer needs, such as a search topic, a data series, a date range, or a Bitcoin address or transaction identifier you ask about, and never your messages or files. A requested address or transaction identifier is sent to BRK, or to Mempool.space if BRK is unavailable. Do not upload passwords, wallet seed phrases, private keys, or information you do not want these processors to handle.

Deleting Agent 21 data

Deleting an individual file also clears the temporary analysis workspace; it does not remove previous answer text or other retained outputs. Use the conversation menu to delete a conversation and its files, or the account controls to request deletion of all Agent 21 data. Deletion immediately hides conversations and blocks further work. A background job then removes chat history, OpenAI sessions and uploaded files, Blob objects, and operational mappings; incomplete cleanup is retried and its status is available in the app. Account removal through Clerk also triggers cleanup through its signed webhook. A deletion receipt containing operational account and job identifiers, but no conversation content may remain for operational verification.

Application deletion does not override a processor's legally required or security-log retention. OpenAI API data handling is governed by its API data controls. Review the policies of Clerk, Neon, and Vercel for their services.

Beta usage

Invited users pay nothing during beta. Secret Satoshis pays the provider charges. Each account runs one response at a time and has file-size and storage limits.

Agent 21 on ChatGPT

The Agent 21 custom GPT runs inside ChatGPT. When it needs data, ChatGPT sends only the parameters for that request to a read-only public service: a Bitcoin series name, index, range, and optional limit to the Bitcoin Research Kit (bitview.space); a public network endpoint and, when you ask for one, a block hash, transaction ID, block height, or Bitcoin address to mempool.space; public tag or market identifiers and discovery filters to Polymarket (gamma-api.polymarket.com); and a public repository owner, name, path, and optional reference to GitHub (api.github.com). Addresses and transaction IDs are pseudonymous but can still be privacy-sensitive; do not submit one you do not want sent to mempool.space.

These Actions use no Agent 21 account, API key, wallet, or login. They cannot write or delete external data, broadcast or accelerate transactions, place prediction-market orders, connect wallets, or access private positions or repositories. Secret Satoshis runs no intermediary server for the GPT and does not receive its requests; they go from ChatGPT to the services above, which may process them under their own policies. Uploaded files and conversations are handled by ChatGPT under your plan, workspace settings, and OpenAI's policies.

Policy updates
